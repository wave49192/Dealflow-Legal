Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

About Dealflow

Dealflow (“Dealflow”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a creator sponsorship workflow and analytics management application. Dealflow is currently primarily used privately. It helps organize creator sponsorship work, including brand deals, campaigns, deliverables, documents, payments, and social-media performance. This policy describes how information is handled in Dealflow and on this legal website.

Information entered into Dealflow

Dealflow stores information voluntarily entered for creator and sponsorship workflows. This may include sponsor and agency names; campaign details; deliverables; briefs, scripts, and drafts; published-post links; pricing and fees; invoices and quotations; tax and payment information; payment and campaign status; publication dates; and notes. These records may include personal information about creators, sponsors, agency contacts, or other people involved in the work. Users should enter only information needed for their workflows and that they are authorized to use.

TikTok information and authorization

When a user explicitly authorizes a TikTok connection through TikTok's official APIs, Dealflow may receive the account or user identifier, display name, username, profile information, follower and following counts, total likes, video count, published-video information, video IDs and URLs, and view, like, comment, and share counts. Other performance metrics may be available through the authorized APIs. The information received depends on the permissions granted and what TikTok makes available.

OAuth access tokens, and refresh tokens where provided, are used to maintain authorized API access. TikTok API credentials and tokens are not exposed publicly. Dealflow does not receive or store the user's TikTok password; TikTok handles authentication.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate Dealflow and its creator and sponsorship workflows. Authorized TikTok information is used only for creator analytics, campaign tracking, sponsorship-performance reporting, and associating social posts with Dealflow work records. Dealflow does not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or run third-party advertising.

Third parties and sharing

TikTok is an independent third-party service. Its own terms and privacy policy govern information it handles. Dealflow is not operated or endorsed by TikTok. Information may be processed by technical service providers as needed to host or operate Dealflow, and may be disclosed where required by law. It is not shared with advertisers. This legal website does not use analytics or tracking cookies; its hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website.

Retention and deletion

Workflow records are retained while needed for the operator's creator and sponsorship work. Authorized TikTok information and tokens are retained while needed for the connected features. Information no longer needed is deleted, except where retention is necessary for legal obligations or legitimate recordkeeping, such as tax or payment records.

You may contact the operator to request deletion of personal information, stored TikTok data, or connection tokens. Requests may require verification of your identity or authority over the relevant account. You can also revoke Dealflow's access through TikTok's connected-app controls. Revoking access prevents further authorized retrieval but does not itself delete information already stored in Dealflow. Deletion from backups, if any, follows their normal expiry cycle.

Security and your choices

The operator takes reasonable precautions to protect information and limit access to authorized use, including keeping API credentials and tokens out of the public legal repository. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You can choose not to connect TikTok and can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting the operator. Applicable legal rights continue to apply.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Dealflow at tumtamai_w@hotmail.com.
